Privacy Notice

Which personal data is processed on this site, why and on what legal ground; your rights and how to apply.

Last updated: 26 September 2026

This is a courtesy translation. The Turkish version is the binding text.

In shortThis site uses no cookies, analytics or forms. I only process your personal data when you e-mail me or apply to exercise a right.

1. Data controller

This notice is given under Article 10 of Türkiye's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) and the Communiqué on the Duty to Inform. The data controller is Ahmet Sencer Zararsız. Contact: avahmetsencerzararsiz@gmail.com.

2. What I process, why, and on what legal ground

Processing activities
ActivityDataPurposeLegal ground
E-mail correspondenceName, e-mail address, anything you include in the message or attachmentsReplying to you and handling the correspondenceLegitimate interest, KVKK Art. 5(2)(f). Where the exchange aims at concluding a contract, KVKK Art. 5(2)(c).

Data is collected electronically, when you send it by e-mail. I do no profiling and no automated decision-making.

Please do not include special categories of data (such as health or criminal records, KVKK Art. 6). If you do, I use them only as far as needed to reply, and otherwise delete them.

3. When you visit the site

I collect no visitor data. Pages set no cookies, load no analytics or ad tools, and fetch nothing (not even fonts) from third-party servers.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc. GitHub's documentation states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor's IP address is logged and stored for security purposes. I have no access to these logs; GitHub decides their purpose and means, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Depending on your choice, two small entries may be written to your browser's local storage. They stay on your device and are never sent to me. See the Cookie Policy.

4. Transfers

I never sell your data or share it for marketing.

My mailbox runs on Google LLC's Gmail service, so messages you send me are stored on the provider's servers outside Türkiye for the purpose of providing the e-mail service. This transfer falls under KVKK Art. 9.

Where needed to establish, exercise or defend a right, or required by law, data may be disclosed to competent authorities and courts under KVKK Art. 8(2)(a).

5. Retention

I keep correspondence for 2 years after the matter is closed and then delete it. Data subject requests and my replies are kept for 2 years from resolution, as evidence. If the reason for processing ends sooner, I delete earlier (KVKK Art. 7).

6. Your rights

Under KVKK Art. 11 you may ask me to: learn whether your data is processed; request information about it; learn the purpose and whether it is used accordingly; know the third parties it was transferred to in Türkiye or abroad; have it corrected; have it erased or destroyed under Art. 7; have corrections or erasures notified to those third parties; object to an adverse outcome resulting solely from automated analysis; and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing.

7. How to apply

Under Art. 5(1) of the Communiqué on Applications to the Data Controller, you may apply in writing, via registered e-mail (KEP), secure electronic or mobile signature, or from an e-mail address you have previously used to write to me, sent to avahmetsencerzararsiz@gmail.com. You can ask for my postal address by e-mail for written applications.

Applications must be in Turkish (Communiqué Art. 4(2)) and include your name (and signature for written applications), Turkish ID number (or nationality and passport/ID number for foreigners), address for notifications, any e-mail/phone/fax, and the subject of your request (Art. 5(2)).

I resolve requests free of charge within 30 days (KVKK Art. 13(2)); a fee of TRY 1 per page may apply beyond 10 pages (Communiqué Art. 7). If your request is refused, the reply is inadequate or late, you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Board within 30 days of learning the reply and in any case within 60 days of applying (KVKK Art. 14(1)).
