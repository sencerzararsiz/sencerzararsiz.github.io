About

After law school, my path ran from courtroom corridors to product teams.

I graduated from Atatürk University Faculty of Law in 2022. I spent my traineeship and first years as a lawyer in Ankara among case files, contracts and data protection documents, and in the same years founded my own ventures and ran legal technology workshops. In 2023 I started coding with HTML, CSS and JavaScript projects on patika.dev; today I can talk to engineering teams in their language and read a product's code to see its legal risk.

The TÖDEB Fintech Programme and my work at PARAM pulled me into payment services, anti-money-laundering rules and regulatory compliance. Today I'm a licensed MASAK Compliance Officer and an ISO/IEC 27001 ISMS Lead Auditor; information security audits, internal control, risk analyses and compliance roadmaps are part of my everyday work.

What I'm really curious about is how law looks and how it's used. At Legalitify I lead the team behind a legaltech product that scans websites for legal compliance, and work on its legal infrastructure. At GameLaw.io, a legal platform for the games ecosystem, I work on legal development, legal engineering and marketing. Through legal design and legaltech training I share this approach with IT and technology communities.

Legal design, live

One clause, two versions.

Legal design makes a text readable and usable without losing legal accuracy. Below is a single paragraph from an e-commerce privacy notice under Türkiye's data protection law (KVKK). Switch between the two versions.

Illustrative example, not legal advice.

In its capacity as data controller, the Company processes your identity and contact data for the purpose of performing your order, on the legal ground set out in Article 5(2)(c) of the Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698, namely that the processing of personal data belonging to the parties to a contract is necessary, provided that it is directly related to the conclusion or performance of that contract; and, limited to that purpose, such data may be transferred to cargo service providers pursuant to Article 8 of the Law. You may submit requests concerning your rights listed in Article 11 of the Law to the Company.

2 sentences106 wordsReadability: hard

To deliver your order, we need a few details from you.

What we use
Your name, address and phone number.
Why
To get your order to you. We don't use it for anything else.
Legal basis
Performance of a contract KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)
Who we share it with
Only the delivery company KVKK Art. 8
Your rights
Access, correction, erasure and objection KVKK Art. 11
